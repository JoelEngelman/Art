ABOUT THE ARTIST

Joel
Engelman.

Making things that
feel like something.

Joel Engelman is a multidisciplinary artist working across modern, abstract and digital art. His practice is driven by curiosity — experimenting with colour, texture, composition and emotion.

Every work starts as an idea and changes along the way. The finished piece is less about having one correct meaning and more about giving the viewer room to create their own.
